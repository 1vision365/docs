# Installation

<!-- agent:begin installation -->
## Requirements

- Business Central version 28.0 or later with PrintVis 28.0 or later.
- [1Vision IoT Link](../iot-link/index.md) and the [1Vision IoT-2-PrintVis Connector](../iot2printvis-connector/index.md), installed and configured.
- Moxa devices that send their data to IoT Link.

## Install the app

1. In Business Central, open **Extension Marketplace**.
2. Search for **1Vision IoT-2-PrintVis Adapter - Moxa** and choose **Get it now**.
3. Follow the prompts and wait for the installation to finish.
4. Open **Extension Management** and check that the status is **Installed**.
5. Assign the [permission set](permissions.md) to your users.
6. Continue with the [configuration](configuration.md).

The installation creates the default tag settings for the Moxa message format and checks the adapters of the connector again.
